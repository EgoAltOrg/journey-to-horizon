The known world. Explored regions are drawn in; the rest is still fog. Hover a marker to see the place, or click through to its page.

```base
views:
  - type: leaflet-map
    name: Map
    image: assets/jth-map.svg
    height: 520
    minZoom: -1
    maxZoom: 3
    defaultZoom: 0
    zoomDelta: 0.25
    scale: "1"
    unit: km
```
