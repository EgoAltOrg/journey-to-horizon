## 2026-11-04

- The six hexes around Marrogate are now on the map: grasslands, badlands and tropical jungle. Everything past them is still fog.
- The map page has a key for the biome symbols, the terrain dots and the shadowblight mark.
- Each explored region on the map now shows the rumor told about it. The Rumors switch in the map's layer menu hides them.
- Marrogate is now a hex on the map: click it to open its page.
- House Rules: planting a cutting from Marrogate's shrine tree joins a place to the Wellspring, for fast travel and long rests, and anyone carrying a Vessel can perform the jump rite.
