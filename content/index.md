A thousand years ago the goddess Aurelia gave her life to save Valloren, and sealed the survivors behind barriers meant to outlast the ruin. The barriers have fallen. The world outside is broken, and waiting.

Journey to Horizon is a weekly, drop-in Daggerheart game run by GEST. Every Wednesday a table sets out across the wounded continent toward the distant Cliffs of Horizon, chasing the ten Soul Echoes that can bring the goddess back.

## How it works

- **Drop in any Wednesday.** No fixed group, no sign-up. Come the weeks you can.
- **Bring a character.** New? A short Session 0 gets you one in an evening. Already have one? Bring them along.
- **Rotating GMs, one shared world.** Different GMs run different nights, but it is one continent and one map that everyone helps fill in.
- **Every night stands alone.** Sessions start and end at a safe sanctuary, so you never need last week's table to enjoy this week's.
