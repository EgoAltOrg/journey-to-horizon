---
title: Terranamancy
---

Every traveler sworn to the journey carries a consecrated Vessel of Light, and every bearer can draw on the Wellspring. That is Terranamancy: channeling terrana, the elemental essence of the land, through the same seven schools the world itself runs on.

## Your Wellspring points

- You have **Wellspring points equal to your level**: one at level 1, up to ten at level 10. (Your level is the march's, see [[house-rules|House Rules]].)
- Spend them across the seven schools. **Each point raises one school by one tier**, and a school's tier is just the points you put into it.
- **A school caps at Tier 4.**
- **You can redistribute all your points whenever the march levels up**, and only then.

So a level-10 bearer might be a specialist (Tier 4 + Tier 4 + Tier 2) or a generalist spread thinner. Your call.

## Channeling a school

You can channel only while standing in a wilderness hex whose habitat matches the school (the table below), and never in a shadowblighted hex or during a Rekindling, where the Wellspring is choked.

1. **Pay the cost:** spend Hope equal to the tier you are channeling. A Tier 3 channel costs 3 Hope.
2. **Roll to hit each enemy separately:** an action roll (2d12 with Hope and Fear) plus your **highest trait**, against that enemy's Difficulty. There is no spellcasting stat here, so no build or class is left out: everyone channels off their best trait.
3. **On each hit** the enemy takes the effect with no save of its own. **Roll the effect's damage die once per tier and total it**, then apply any rider condition. A Tier 3 Heat channel that hits rolls 3d6; a miss does nothing to that enemy. Stone is a self-buff and needs no roll (see the table).

Two conditions are turned to face enemies instead of you, each lasting until the end of that enemy's next turn:

- **Frostbitten** (from Ice): the target attacks with disadvantage.
- **Nauseated** (from Poison): the target is Vulnerable.

## The seven schools

| School | Range | On a successful channel |
|---|---|---|
| **Heat** | Very Close | d6 direct magic damage (per tier); the target marks a Stress. |
| **Overgrowth** | Close | d8 physical damage (per tier); the target is Restrained until it breaks free with a Strength Roll. |
| **Wind** | Close | d8 magic damage (per tier); the target is pushed to Far. Until you take damage, attacks against you have disadvantage. |
| **Ice** | Close, in front of you | d10 magic damage (per tier); the target is Frostbitten. |
| **Poison** | Close | d6 direct magic damage (per tier); the target is Nauseated. |
| **Stone** | Self | You add your Stone tier to your damage thresholds until you take Severe damage or channel Stone again. No roll. |
| **Water** | Very Close | The target is pushed to Close and marks Stress equal to the tier. |

## Where each school works

Habitat is rolled once per region, and a region spans several hexes, so a matching region gives you sustained use of its school, not a single hex.

| Habitat | School |
|---|---|
| Shadowblighted | none (the Wellspring is choked) |
| Underground, Badlands | Stone |
| Aquatic | Water |
| Wetland | Poison |
| Grassland, Rolling | Wind |
| Tropical, Forest | Overgrowth |
| Drylands | Heat |
| Mountain, Frozen | Ice |
