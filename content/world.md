The continent of Valloren, a thousand years after the goddess fell, and the journey to bring her back.

## Valloren and the goddess

Long ago the continent of Valloren was shaped by the goddess **Aurelia**, who wielded **terrana**, the elemental essence that runs through all living land. When a betrayal among her own threatened to extinguish magic, Aurelia gave her life to call down the stars and burn the corruption away. To keep the survivors from being lost with it, she raised barriers around their sanctuaries that would hold for a thousand years.

## The Long Sorrow

Behind the barriers, the faithful waited out the centuries. Outside them, terrana sank into hiding and the color drained from the land: the slow ruin called the **Long Sorrow**. Now the barriers have come down, and the sanctuaries open onto a New Valloren that is turbulent and still dangerous.

## The quest

To restore the world is to restore its goddess. Ten **Soul Echoes** sleep in ten shrines across the continent. Gathered and carried to the **Cliffs of Horizon** in the far northeast, they can resurrect Aurelia. The road runs from the sanctuary of Marrogate in the southwest all the way to Horizon, one shrine at a time.

## The Wellspring

As the shrines are relit, the Wellspring rises: the buried network of terrana finding its way back to the surface, and the land around a reawakened shrine flushes green again. Every traveler sworn to the journey carries a **Vessel of Light**, a small consecrated object that kindles brighter as the march nears its goal. The magic drawn from the Wellspring is [[terranamancy|Terranamancy]].

## What stands in the way

- **Shadowblight:** the corruption Aurelia died fighting, still choking the land in crystal blight and birthing Colossi that march on the sanctuaries to devour their light.
- **The Cult of the Two-Headed Coin:** those who would keep the goddess dead, seizing shrines and their Echoes for their own ends.
- **The Seekers-in-Shadow:** drawn to the light of a Vessel, hunting those who carry the Echoes.

How the march reclaims each Echo from what holds it is the [[house-rules|Rekindling]].
